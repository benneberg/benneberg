## Selected work
### Insidr
Remote development, diagnostics and telemetry for locked-down Chromium-based edge devices.
Exploring reliable communication, device observability and remote engineering workflows at the edge.
### OmniSign / ScreenMesh
An architecture for resilient digital signage built around a separation between **cloud control planes** and **edge execution**.
### ShadowFrame
A high-fidelity laboratory environment for experimenting with and simulating signage edge runtimes without requiring physical devices.
### ARIP
**AI Repository Intelligence Platform**
An exploration of how repositories can become machine-readable systems through structural analysis, ASTs, embeddings and AI-assisted understanding.
### Infographic Studio  
An experiment in deterministic, programmatic visual generation — exploring how useful visual artifacts can be generated from structured information without relying entirely on  generative AI.

## Experimental ideas
Some of my projects start as engineering problems.
Others start with a question.
I'm particularly interested in exploring ideas around:
- **AI Workforce OS** — treating AI systems as organized workforces with roles, responsibilities, capabilities and accountability.
- **Predixa** — making advanced AI prediction capabilities understandable and actionable for non-technical business decision makers.
- **Intent Runtime** — exploring software architectures where intent becomes a first-class input rather than simply a sequence of commands.
- **Human–AI systems** — investigating what becomes possible when AI is treated as a collaborator within a larger system rather than simply a chatbot.
Not every experiment becomes a product.
That's part of the point.
